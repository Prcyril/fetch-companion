CTA hierarchy updated

Fetch uses black for primary actions, pink for selections/accent only. This is the most important thing to carry into the build.

Primary: black pill · Secondary: white outline pill · Selected: pink pill · All full-width within 20px screen padding · fit-content when inline

Detail chips updated

Fetch uses auto-width pill chips for metadata — content hugs text. The Mac screenshots made these look full-width but on mobile they're compact.
